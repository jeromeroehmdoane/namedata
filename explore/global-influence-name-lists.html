Global Influence Name Lists

These are the curated name lists used in the Global Influence visualizations. They are intended to illustrate broad patterns in recorded U.S. baby names, not to classify individual people.

Important: A person's name does not determine their religion, ancestry, nationality, or migration history. These lists are unofficial, are likely incomplete, and inevitably omit or simplify names that cross cultures and traditions.
