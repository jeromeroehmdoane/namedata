STEP 2 — RESPONSIVE VIEWER PATCH
================================

Upload/replace these three files in the namedata repository:

  explore/view.html
  explore/explore.css
  explore/catalog.js

No visualization HTML files need to be replaced for this patch.

What this patch does
--------------------
- Fixed-width Plotly exports are rendered at their original design width and
  proportionally scaled down inside the viewer when the available width is
  smaller. They are no longer clipped.
- The Draw a Name Curve application remains fluid rather than being shrunk.
- The viewer measures same-origin iframe content and expands its height when
  responsive wrapping makes the visualization taller.
- The standalone visualization links remain unchanged.

Suggested commit message:
  Make visualization viewer responsive

Test at narrow widths:
  /explore/view.html?viz=cultural-name-waves
  /explore/view.html?viz=top-name-by-state&variant=female
  /explore/view.html?viz=draw-name-curve
